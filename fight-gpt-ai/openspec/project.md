# Project: Fight GPT

## AI-Powered Fighting Game Coaching Platform

### Project Specification Document

## 1. Project Overview

`Fight GPT` is a next-generation, AI-powered coaching platform for the global fighting game community (FGC). It is designed as a subscription-based (SaaS) application for web (React) and mobile (Flutter).

The core mission is to **compress the learning curve** of competitive fighting games. The platform acts as a personal "Sensei" that does the homework for the player by scraping game mechanics and analyzing thousands of professional-level YouTube videos.

It provides personalized, actionable training plans, answers complex strategic questions, and helps players stay current with the evolving "meta." This makes competitive play accessible and fun for everyone, from new players to returning veterans who no longer have time for the "grind."

## 2. The Problem & Value Proposition

### The Problem

- **Steep Learning Curve:** Fighting games are notoriously difficult to master, requiring memorization of vast amounts of frame data, matchups, and execution-heavy combos.
- **Massive Time Commitment:** The "grind" of labbing, watching VODs (Video on Demand), and researching strategies is a significant barrier for players with jobs, families, and limited time.
- **Static Training Modes:** In-game training modes are static. They don't teach the evolving, community-driven "meta," advanced strategies, or how to adapt to different playstyles.
- **Player Drop-off:** New players often get discouraged by the "knowledge gap," while returning veterans feel too far behind the current meta to compete.

### Our Solution & Value

- **Compress the Learning Curve:** The AI does the 40 hours of homework so the player can focus on 15-minute, high-impact practice sessions.
- **Empower the "Comeback" Player:** We directly target players who feel "left behind." Our AI finds the most important new strategies and delivers them as simple "quests," making it easy to catch up.
- **Gamified Progression:** We make practice engaging. By framing training as an RPG—with "quests," "XP," and "leveling up" a character's mastery—we make the learning process addictive instead of a chore.

## 3. Core Features

### v1.0: The Meta-Coach

- **Multi-Game Support:** The platform is architected to be game-agnostic. The user selects their primary game (e.g., *Street Fighter 6*, *Tekken 8*, *Smash Bros. Ultimate*).
- **AI Meta-Analysis Engine:**
  - **Game Mechanics Scraper:** A background worker that scrapes community wikis (e.g., *SuperCombo*, *Dustloop*) for character mechanics, move lists, and frame data.
  - **Pro VOD Analyzer:** A background worker that scans YouTube for new tournament/pro-level footage, analyzes it with `MediaPipe` and `OpenCV`, and identifies patterns, combos, and strategies.
- **Personalized Coaching Dashboard:**
  - The user selects their "main" character.
  - The AI provides a personalized "quest log" of daily/weekly drills based on the current pro-level meta for that character.
  - **Example:** "The new meta for Ryu involves this 3-hit corner combo. Today's quest: Practice it 10 times."
- **"Ask the AI" (RAG):** A chatbot where users can ask natural language questions ("How do I punish Ken's Dragon Punch?") and receive expert answers generated by an LLM using the scraped frame data and video analysis as its knowledge source.

### v2.0: The Personal Sensei

- **Personal Gameplay Analysis:** The platform's ultimate feature.
  - Users can link their YouTube channel or upload their own match replays/videos.
  - The AI analyzes the *user's* gameplay, identifying their specific habits, weaknesses, and missed opportunities.
  - **Example:** "The AI watched your 5 matches. It noticed you successfully anti-aired only 2/10 times. Today's quest: Complete this 10-minute anti-air drill."

## 4. Technical Architecture

The project employs a scalable **microservice architecture**.

- **Frontend (Web):** `React`
  - A modern, responsive dashboard with a high-end "gaming" theme.
- **Frontend (Mobile):** `Flutter`
  - A cross-platform mobile app for on-the-go coaching and quest tracking.
- **Backend API Gateway (Port 5000):** `Node.js (Express)`
  - Manages user accounts, authentication, subscriptions, and all client-facing API requests.
  - Communicates with the Python AI Service and the database to generate coaching plans.
- **AI Analysis Service (Port 8000):** `Python (FastAPI)`
  - A **Dockerized** microservice that handles all heavy lifting.
  - **Modules:** `OpenCV` (video processing), `MediaPipe` (pose analysis), `yt-dlp` (video download), `BeautifulSoup4` (scraping).
  - Receives job requests from the Node.js backend (e.g., "Analyze this URL").
- **Database:** `MongoDB`
  - A NoSQL database to store all game, character, frame data, analysis results, and user profiles. Serves as the central "brain" for the AI.

## 5. Globalization & Localization

The FGC is global. The platform will be built from the ground up for localization.

- **Priority Languages:** English, Japanese, Spanish, French, Korean.
- **Strategy:** This is not just text translation. The AI models will be **regionally trained**. To coach a Japanese user, the AI will be trained by watching Japanese pro VODs and scraping Japanese-language wikis to ensure cultural relevance and a legitimate, high-quality coaching experience.

## 6. Monetization Model

- **Tier 1: Rookie (Free):** 1 game, 1 character, basic guides, community access.
- **Tier 2: Contender (Pro):** All games, unlimited characters, AI meta-analysis, "Ask the AI," personalized coaching quests.
- **Tier 3: World Warrior (Elite):** All Pro features, plus the v2.0 Personal Gameplay Analysis (VOD/replay upload).
